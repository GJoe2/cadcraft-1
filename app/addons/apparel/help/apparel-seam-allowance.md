## The allowance

A piece keeps its **sew line**; the **cut line** is drawn round it at the seam allowance and is drawn again after every change. A piece with no allowance is a net pattern: its cut line is the sew line.

- New pieces get the width in {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:CadCraft}} › {{ui:Apparel}} › {{ui:Seam allowance for new pieces}} (10 mm, or 1/2" in a drawing in inches), or the one you type at {{cmd:PIECE}}.
- Change it for the whole piece in {{ui:Properties}} › {{ui:Seam allowance}}.

## Corners

Where the cut lines of two edges meet, the corner can:

| Rule | The cut |
|---|---|
| Follow | the two cut lines run on until they meet (the usual corner) |
| Square | the wider allowance is cut off square to its edge |
| Mitre | straight across, from where one cut line ends to where the next begins |
| Reverse | turned back: the wider allowance (a hem) is folded back over the sew line, so its end follows the next edge's cut line as a mirror image; the hem then fits when it is turned up |

Inside corners always trim the two cut lines where they cross.
