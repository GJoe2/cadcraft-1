## Not built yet

- **Risers and drops:** a run stays at one elevation; draw a vertical run as separate runs at their own elevations.
- **Fitting pressure losses**, static pressure totals, branch balancing and fan selection: sizing is by friction rate only.
- **Air terminals** (diffusers, grilles, registers), dampers, fire dampers, access doors and equipment: draw them as blocks for now.
- **Duct gauges, reinforcement and hangers** by construction standard, and insulation or lining thickness.
- **Branches that follow their main** when the main moves: draw the branch to the main again.
- **Splitting a run** at a point, or **joining** two runs into one: draw it again.
- **Ducts on sheets** other than through viewports, and **sections and elevations** cut from the 3D ductwork.
- **Spanish words** for the add-on: they come with the next language update.
