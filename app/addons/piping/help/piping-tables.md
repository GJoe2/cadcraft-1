## The tables

- **Steel pipe** — ASME B36.10M, Welded and Seamless Wrought Steel Pipe: NPS 1/8 to 24, outside diameter, and the wall of Schedule 40 and 80. NPS 2 Schedule 40 is 2.375 in outside, 0.154 in wall, 2.067 in inside; DN 50 in a metric drawing.
- **Copper water tube** — ASTM B88: 1/4 to 4, outside diameter 1/8 in more than the nominal size, walls of types K, L and M (type M is not made in 1/4 and 5/8). 3/4 type L is 0.875 in outside, 0.045 in wall.
- **PVC pipe** — ASTM D1785: NPS 1/2 to 12, the same outside diameters as steel, the standard's minimum walls for Schedule 40 and 80.
- **PEX tubing** — ASTM F876, copper tube sizes, SDR 9: outside diameter 1/8 in more than the nominal size, wall one ninth of it but at least 0.070 in.
- **Elbows** — the long-radius 90° elbow of ASME B16.9 sets the bend steel elbows are drawn with.

Metric figures are the same pipe in millimeters. The add-on draws, sizes and counts from these tables; it does not choose a pipe for a pressure, temperature or flow: that is the designer's call.
