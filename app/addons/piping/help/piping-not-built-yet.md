## Not built yet

- **Risers**: a run stays in plan with one slope; draw a vertical run as separate runs at their own elevations.
- **Pipe sizing** from flow, pressure drop or fixture units, and **hanger** spacing.
- **More materials and standards**: stainless steel, cast iron soil pipe, ductile iron, CPVC, HDPE, metric copper to EN 1057, and fittings' own dimensions for copper, PVC and PEX (their bends are drawn at one outside diameter).
- **Joints** (welds, solder, threads, solvent) counted in the schedule; **insulation**.
- **Valves on the 3D tab**: valves show and count in 2D; the solid is the pipe.
- **Branches that follow their main** when the main moves: draw the branch to the main again.
- **Isometric drawings** from the runs.
- **Spanish words** for the add-on: they come with the next language update.
